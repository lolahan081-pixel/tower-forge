# Next short steps

1. **Skill gems + support links.** Socket a skill gem on a tower and link supports that feed into `resolveStats` (and later extra effects). Data-driven gem defs, same id-lookup pattern as towers.
2. **Affixed loot.** Enemies drop items with a few affixes that modify a `StatBlock` or add a simple on-hit effect. Inventory is a short list; click to equip on a tower.
3. **Passive tree.** A small node graph granting global damage, rate, range, gold, or life. Spend points earned from clearing waves.
4. **Crafting.** One bench: reroll or add an affix on a piece of loot, paid in gold (and later, shards).
5. **Enemy modifiers.** Wave or pack prefixes/suffixes (fast, tanky, resistant, enraged) as data entries applied when spawning, not special-case waves.
